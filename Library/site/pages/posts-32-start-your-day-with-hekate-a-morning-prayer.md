## Текст страницы

In the gentle glow of the morning light, we praise Hekate, Goddess of crossroads and magick. As we start a new day, we call on Her, asking for guidance and wisdom for our journey. To light our way, give us Her ancient knowledge, and help us face life’s challenges with courage and purpose. In Her grace, we honor Hekate and offer our love and devotion, inviting Her blessings to guide us throughout the day.

“Glorious Hekate, Lady of the Crossroads, Trivia, Queen of Night, Torchbearer, Mistress of Magic, and Guardian of the Underworld, I call upon You at the dawn of this new day, seeking Your guidance and wisdom as I embark upon the journey ahead.

As Lady of the Crossroads, show me the path I must take today, and grant me the courage to walk it with determination and purpose.

As Trivia, share with me Your wisdom and insight, so that I may make wise decisions and discern truth from falsehood.

As Queen of Night, shine Your light into the darkness of my soul, illuminating the hidden depths of my being and revealing my true purpose.

As Torchbearer, light my way through the shadows of doubt and uncertainty, and lead me to the place where my heart finds peace.

As Mistress of Magick, empower me to manifest my desires, and to use my will to create positive change in my life and in the world around me.

As Guardian of the Underworld, watch over me as I journey through the challenges of this day, and protect me from harm.

Great Hekate, I honor You with these words and offer my love and devotion. May Your blessings be upon me always.”
