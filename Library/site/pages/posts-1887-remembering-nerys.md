## Текст страницы

My heart aches with a profound sorrow that words cannot fully express. Today, my beloved feline companion, Nerys (pronounced “nur-reece”), crossed the Rainbow Bridge. She passed away in my arms, her spirit gently slipping away into the eternal embrace of Hekate.

Nerys was more than just a pet; she was a cherished member of my family. A loyal friend, a purring comfort, and a source of endless joy. She brought warmth to my life, a soft glow that illuminated even the darkest days.

Nerys had been sick for a while, battling her illness with remarkable strength and grace. Despite her condition, she continued to bring joy and love into my life every day. She had turned 18 years old in August, and I was hoping she’d at least make it to January, so we could share the holidays together, but it seems her journey had to end sooner.

As she joins her sisters, Eben and Skaara, in the spirit realm, I find solace in the knowledge that they are together once more. Right now, through the tears in my eyes, I imagine her playing tag with her sisters and engaging in the playful roughhousing that cats love, all under the watchful gaze of Hekate. They are now free from the constraints of this mortal coil, exploring the boundless fields of eternity.

Though grief weighs heavy upon my heart, I am grateful for the time we shared. Her memory will forever be etched in my soul, a reminder of the deep love and connection we shared.

In honor of Nerys’s memory, I encourage you to hold your own furry companions a little closer. Give them an extra dose of love today: a gentle pet, a tasty treat, or simply a few moments of undivided attention. If you don’t have a pet, consider donating to a local humane society or no-kill animal shelter to help those in need. Life is short and can end unexpectedly; let’s cherish every precious moment we have with our beloved animals and show kindness to all creatures, great and small.

Hail, Nerys. May your spirit soar free among the stars. I love you beyond what words can express and miss you with every beat of my heart.

Nerys
2006 – 2024
