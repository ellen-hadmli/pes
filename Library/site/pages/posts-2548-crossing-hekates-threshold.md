## Текст страницы

If you have been following Hail Hekate for a while, you have probably noticed the dead air. It has been a long time since I posted a new ritual, prayer, or substantial update here, but I have not been taking a break from the work. I have been dismantling my practice down to the studs and rebuilding it from the ground up because Hekate demanded it.

She forced me to examine the system I had spent years constructing and rebuild it according to what She revealed through sustained contact. Along the way, She completely reshaped my understanding of reality, witchcraft, divine communication, and, most importantly, exactly who and what She is. What began as the reconstruction of my private practice eventually became something much larger: a complete system of Hekatean witchcraft designed to help others build a direct, sustained relationship with Her.

I have now officially launched the Hail Hekate Patreon, including my new on-line course, Crossing Hekate’s Threshold.

The course contains teachings Hekate revealed to me through years of direct work with Her. Some confirmed things I had sensed for a long time. Others completely dismantled what I thought I understood about Her nature, consciousness, witchcraft, divine guidance, and the structure beneath the reality we experience. I have spent this quiet period testing those teachings, translating them from my private grimoire, and shaping them into a system another practitioner can actually follow and use.

Without giving away material that belongs inside the course, I will tell you that Hekate led me toward the epithet that most clearly expresses Her nature: Azostos, the Unbound. If I were forced to strip away every accumulated image and name the three titles that best reveal the magnitude of Her power, they would be Azostos, Anima Mundi, and Soteira. Understanding why changed the entire structure of my practice.

The effects did not remain confined to my altar. This work forced a massive reconstruction of my life. Old arrangements began collapsing, decisions I had postponed became impossible to avoid, and the future I had resigned myself to started breaking open. Serious practice eventually reaches beyond prayer and ritual. It exposes where your life has become stagnant, where you have accepted less than you are capable of, and where the structure surrounding you can no longer support the person you are becoming.

That is the body of work now opening through Patreon. There are four tiers, each offering a different way to enter.

The Prayers Tier
The Prayers Tier gives you immediate access to a growing collection of prayers written for devotion, guidance, protection, healing, spiritual growth, manifestation, and deeper communion with Hekate. These prayers come from my own practice and are intended to be spoken, experienced, and returned to whenever they serve a meaningful place in yours.

Each prayer also includes something I call the Witch’s Communion. This is the deeper method I use to move beyond simply reading words aloud and enter fully into the prayer itself. Through visualization, posture, breath, offerings, focused actions, and other devotional techniques, the Witch’s Communion transforms the prayer into an active spiritual experience and helps carry the power of that contact beyond the moment in which the words are spoken.

New prayers and Witch’s Communions will be added every month, allowing the collection to continue growing alongside your practice.

The Rituals Tier
The Rituals Tier includes everything available in the Prayers Tier, along with a growing library of fully structured, step-by-step rituals drawn from my personal grimoire.

These rites cover devotion, purification, protection, healing, manifestation, consecration, empowerment, spiritual development, and deeper engagement with Hekate. Some may become permanent foundations of your practice, while others are designed to meet a particular need, transition, or stage of your path.

The rituals are written to be clear, functional, and usable within an ordinary human life. I have no interest in burying effective witchcraft beneath ceremonial clutter simply to make it look advanced. The rites may grow as the practitioner develops, but their depth comes from what they accomplish and reveal, rather than how many obscure tools you can balance on an altar without knocking the whole fucking thing over.

New rituals will be added every month, along with continued additions to the prayer collection.

The Tarot Tier
The Tarot Tier includes the complete prayer and ritual libraries, along with a private monthly tarot reading from me.

I personally pull the cards for each member, guided by Hekate and backed by twenty years of experience behind the deck. You may submit a specific question about your life, spiritual practice, relationships, work, decisions, obstacles, or another situation requiring clarity. If you do not have a particular question, you may request a general outlook for the coming thirty days.

These readings are diagnostic and practical. We examine the forces currently shaping your circumstances, the blockages interfering with your progress, developing strengths, blind spots, and the actions that may help you move forward. You will not receive three generic cards wrapped in vague encouragement and launched into your Patreon messages like a spiritual fortune cookie.

Your completed reading is delivered privately through Patreon each month.

The Course Tier: Crossing Hekate’s Threshold
The Course Tier is the center of the Patreon and the deepest level of engagement with the work.

When you join, you receive immediate access to Lesson One of Crossing Hekate’s Threshold, along with the complete growing libraries of prayers, Witch’s Communions, and rituals. A new course lesson is then delivered directly to your email every two weeks for as long as your Course Tier membership remains active.

The course unfolds in phases, beginning with the devotional, practical, and spiritual foundation required to build sustained contact with Hekate. At the end of the first phase, we go directly to Her for a progress report identifying weaknesses, blind spots, and the areas of your practice that still require attention. From there, the course continues into the next stage of the work, building upon everything established before it.

The heart of the course is the body of teaching Hekate gave me through years of sustained contact with Her. The rituals provide a practical way to encounter those teachings through your own work, but the real draw is what She revealed to me over the past year about Her nature, consciousness, divine communication, witchcraft, reality, and the forces operating beneath spiritual practice. Some of those teachings completely shattered my previous understanding and forced me to rebuild the entire framework through which I approached Her.

This course is not only for beginners. You may already pray, make offerings, perform rituals, and know that Hekate is present in your life, yet still feel as though your practice has stopped moving or never developed into the connection you were trying to build. Crossing Hekate’s Threshold is designed to give both new and experienced practitioners the structure, teachings, and sustained pressure needed to move beyond that stagnation.

The work can change far more than what happens at your altar, but only if you actually do it. I can give you the structure, the lessons, and the tools Hekate gave me. I cannot perform the practice on your behalf. Approach it seriously, maintain the work, and allow it to challenge the life you have built, and the results can be profound. Half-ass it, and I cannot promise you a damn thing.

The lessons arrive in sequence because each one prepares you to understand what follows. Handing someone the entire course on the first day would encourage them to leap toward whatever appears darkest or most impressive while ignoring the foundation required to comprehend it. I refuse to turn this work into another digital vault people download, skim, and abandon in a folder containing two hundred other occult PDFs.

Membership is paid month to month through Patreon. You are never required to pay for the entire course upfront, and you may cancel whenever you choose. You remain in control of whether the material continues to serve you rather than handing over several hundred dollars before you have even read the first lesson and later discovering you bought months of someone talking in circles.

The Course Tier also includes direct access to my personal guidance. If you have a question about a lesson, encounter a problem with the ritual mechanics, or need help understanding something arising through the work, you can contact me directly. I respond within 24 hours.

Because that guidance comes personally from me, enrollment in the Course Tier will be limited. I cannot give serious attention to an unlimited number of practitioners without eventually reducing that guidance to rushed, half-assed replies, and I will not do that. Once the available places are filled, the tier will close and new students may have to wait for an opening.

The existing material on Hail Hekate is not disappearing. Every prayer, ritual, and article already published here will remain freely available, and I will continue adding new public content. Patreon is where the deeper, structured body of work now lives and where those who wish to support Hail Hekate can gain access to everything I have been building during this long period of silence.

I know how claims of direct instruction from a Goddess sound. Spend ten minutes in the online occult world and you will trip over someone announcing that a deity told them to start a podcast, change their username, or purchase a particular brand of incense. Skepticism is reasonable. I approached my own experiences with the same skepticism.

I did not build this course around one dramatic vision or a convenient moment of spiritual certainty. I brought my hardest questions to Hekate, tested what I received through practice, and watched the answers hold under sustained use. The contact deepened, the structure continued revealing itself, and my life changed around it.

Now I have the privilege of handing you the blueprints.

If you are prepared to deepen your relationship with Hekate and enter the teachings She gave me through years of direct work with Her, join me on Patreon.

The first lesson of Crossing Hekate’s Threshold is waiting.

Cross the threshold.
