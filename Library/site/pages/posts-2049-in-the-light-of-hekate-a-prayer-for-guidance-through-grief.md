## Текст страницы

In the stillness of grief, we turn to Hekate, Keeper of the Keys and Guardian of Thresholds. As we navigate the painful path of loss, we call upon Her wisdom and compassion to guide us through the darkness. With Her light, may we find strength in our sorrow and honor the spirits of those we grieve. In reverence, we offer this prayer, seeking Hekate’s presence to help us transform our pain into remembrance and our hearts into beacons of love that transcend death.

“Hekate, Radiant Torchbearer, Beloved Keeper of the Keys, I come before You with a heart heavy with the ache of loss. In the shadowed corridors of grief, I call upon Your guiding light, for You are the Guardian of Thresholds, the steady hand that leads us through the mysteries of life and death.

Compassionate Hekate, Who walks beside the sorrowful and hears the silent cries of the soul, enfold me in Your boundless embrace. Let Your torches blaze through the darkness, not only to illuminate the strength within it, but to bring solace to my weary heart. Transform my anguish into a light that honors what I’ve lost, so the love I’ve known may never fade.

Triple-faced Goddess, Eternal Witness to All Beginnings and Endings, help me honor the bonds that death cannot sever. Teach me to let go of what I cannot carry, yet hold with reverence the essence of what once was. In Your liminal spaces, where the veil thins, let my tears fall freely as sacred offerings, cleansing my spirit and connecting me to the eternal.

Hekate Chthonia, Shepherdess of Souls, may those I grieve find sanctuary in Your care. May Your light guide them gently on their journey, and may I feel their presence in the quiet moments… the rustle of leaves, the glow of the moon, and the stillness of my heart, eternal and unwavering. Remind me that love is never truly lost, but carried forward in the cycles You so faithfully guard.

Keeper of the Veil, grant me the courage to walk this path of sorrow with grace. Let me rise, gradually and with strength, bearing the scars of grief as sacred marks of love. Help me see that even in endings, there are seeds of new beginnings, and that through You, all paths lead home.

Hekate, My Guide, My Solace, I offer You my pain, my tears, my devotion. Transform them in Your infinite wisdom into strength, peace, and everlasting love. With a heart full of gratitude for Your presence, I honor You in this moment and in all moments to come.

Blessed be Your eternal flame, Your boundless compassion, and Your sacred presence, which illuminates the deepest shadows and leads all souls home. Blessed be the path You walk with me, through the valleys of sorrow and the peaks of solace, through the darkness of loss and the radiant light of healing. May Your wisdom lift me from grief and restore my spirit to the peace and strength that resides within me, now and forevermore.”
