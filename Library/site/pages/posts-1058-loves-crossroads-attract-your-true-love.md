## Текст страницы

As hearts flutter with longing, the new moon ignites a spark of hope. We embark on a magickal quest to guide that spark towards a love that resonates deeply within our souls. Tonight, with a touch of witchcraft and the blessings of Hekate, we unlock the desires nestled within your heart, attracting a love that aligns with your authentic self and ignites your soul’s fire. Shed the light on your yearning, for in this sacred space you open yourself to a connection as true and radiant as the promise of a new dawn. Let this ritual unfold, bathing you in Hekate’s transformative power and leading you towards paths where love awaits, ready to embrace you with open arms.

Here’s what you’ll need: 

- A glass spray bottle

- Rose water

- Lavender essential oil

- Another sweet-smelling essential oil of your choice (such as vanilla, jasmine, or ylang-ylang)

- A funnel

- A black candle

- A white candle 

- A red candle

- Lavender incense

- A key

- A piece of paper and a pen 

- An offering

This ritual should be perfomed during a new moon. The new moon represents the beginning of a new cycle, a fresh start, and a renewal of energy. This is a good time to set your intentions for love and manifest them in your life.

First, you need to charge the rose water with the power of the full moon. This is an important step that needs to be done in advance, preferably on the full moon before the new moon. To do this, fill a glass jar or bottle with rose water and close it tightly. Place it on a windowsill or outside where it can receive the moonlight. Say a prayer or affirmation to Hekate, asking Her to infuse the water with Her lunar energy and love power. Leave it overnight, or for as long as you can, until the next morning.

Next, you need to make the “love potion” spray. To do this, you will need a glass spray bottle, a funnel, lavender essential oil, and another sweet-smelling essential oil of your choice. Using the funnel, pour about 1/4 cup of the charged rose water into the spray bottle. Add 10 drops of lavender essential oil and 10 drops of the other essential oil to the bottle. Shake well to combine the ingredients. Label the bottle with a name or a symbol that represents love to you.

Set up your altar or workspace. Place the black candle on the left, the white candle on the right, and the red candle in the center. Place the incense in front of the red candle, and the key behind it. Leave some space for the offering and the paper.

Light the candles and the incense, and say:

“Hekate, Goddess of Magick, Crossroads, and the Underworld, I invoke You in this sacred hour of darkness. Hekate Enodia, Who presides over the meeting of unseen paths, lead me to a love that is true to my soul and pure in intention. Hekate Kleidoukhos, Who holds the keys to the hidden realms, align my heart with the rhythm of love’s secret dance. Hekate Phosphoros, Who shines with the fire of the stars, light the way to a genuine bond that transcends the surface. Hekate Chthonia, Who knows the wisdom of the depths, favor me in this rite that seeks Your guidance and blessing.”

Now, you need to bless the love potion spray. To do this, hold the bottle in your hands and say:

“Hekate, Goddess of Moonlit Paths and Entwined Fates, gather Your magick close. Imbue this potion with Your charm, a love song for my truest self to hear. Hekate Enodia, Weaver of Destinies, guide my steps towards love’s embrace. Let this fragrant potion lead me to a connection as genuine as the moonlight. Hekate Kleidoukhos, unlock the treasure hidden within my heart. Open me to love that is pure and true, a reflection of my deepest desires. Hekate Phosphoros, bathe me in the radiant glow of Your lovelight. Let this essence shimmer, attracting a love that ignites my soul with passion. Hekate Chthonia, nourish the love I seek within. Let it blossom and bloom, filling my life with joy and fulfillment. As I mist myself with this offering, my heart opens to love’s embrace. Hekate, guide me, bless me, and lead me to my true love’s waiting face.“

Write down on the paper what kind of love you are looking for, and what qualities you desire in a partner. Be as specific and honest as you can, but avoid using names or personal details of anyone you know. This is not a spell to manipulate or coerce anyone, but to attract someone who is compatible and harmonious with you.

Fold the paper and place it under the red candle. Say: 

“Hekate, I offer You this petition from the depths of my soul, I ask You to help me find the love that matches my essence, I ask You to open the doors of possibility and fate. You Who see all the paths that lie before me, You Who know all the secrets that lie within me, You Who guide me through the darkness and the light. You Who hold the keys to the mysteries of the cosmos, You Who unlock the potential of the human spirit, You Who reveal the hidden treasures of the earth. You Who ignite the fire of passion and creativity, You Who inspire the spark of love and attraction, You Who enlighten the mind and the soul. May Your gifts reveal to me the embrace of love that awaits in the shadows.”

Take the key and hold it in your dominant hand. Visualize a bright light emanating from it, and say:

“Hekate Kleidoukhos, great Goddess of Keys, Doors, and Secrets, I hold this symbol of Your power in my hand. I ask You to unlock the potential within me. I ask You to reveal the hidden paths to my happiness. I ask You to open the doors of love for me, and bring me the one who is meant for me.“

Visualize yourself being happy and loved by your ideal partner. Feel the warmth and joy of being in a loving relationship. Imagine the key glowing with a pink light and attracting love to you.

After placing the key back on the altar, give Hekate your offerings. Then say:

“Hail Hekate, Goddess of Transformation, Renewal, and Tebirth! You Who hold the keys to the mysteries of life and death. You Who guide the souls of the departed and the seekers of the truth. You Who bless the witches with Your wisdom and power, I thank You for Your presence and assistance. I honor You with this offering of gratitude and devotion. May our bond be strong and our paths be clear.”

As you extinguish the black and white candles, let the red candle glow until it dies.

Carry the paper with you until your heart’s desire comes true, then release it to the fire or the earth as a token of gratitude. Hold on to the key as a charm of Hekate’s power to draw love into your life.

To use the blessed love potion, spray it on yourself during the ritual to enhance your attraction and confidence. You can also spray it on the paper where you wrote your love intention to amplify its power, and on the key to charge it with love energy. After the ritual, use this spray every day as a way to attract love into your life and remind yourself of your intention.

May Hekate’s blessings guide you on your journey to love and fulfillment.
