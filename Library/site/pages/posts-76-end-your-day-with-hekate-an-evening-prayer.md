## Текст страницы

In the embrace of the night, beneath the canopy of stars, we invoke the mighty Hekate, Goddess of the crossroads and guardian of liminal spaces. As we enter the dream world, we give Her our hearts and minds, asking for protection, guidance, and insight. To lead us in the dark, reveal new paths, and shine the light that clears doubt. In Hekate’s name, we express gratitude, devotion, and a request for Her blessings in the night.

“Mighty Hekate, revered Goddess of the crossroads, Guardian of the liminal spaces and the mysteries of night, I come before You with a humble heart and open mind.

As I prepare to rest my body and mind in sleep, I ask for Your protection and guidance through the night. Watch over me as I journey through the realms of dreams, and grant me the clarity and insight to understand their messages.

Lady of the Underworld, Keeper of the Keys, guide me safely through the darkness and the unknown. Illuminate my path with Your torches of wisdom, and reveal to me the hidden truths that lie beyond.

Hekate of the Three Ways, Divine Protector, I entrust myself to Your care and Your mercy. Grant me the strength to face any challenges that may come, and lead me to the light of a new dawn.

Propylaia, Goddess of the Gate, open the way for me and lead me to new opportunities. Let Your presence clear the obstacles from my path, and let Your blessings shower upon me like a gentle rain.

Phosphoros, Bringer of Light, shine Your light upon me and guide me on my path. Let Your radiance dispel the darkness, and let Your clarity help me see through the haze.

Hekate, in Your name I offer my gratitude and devotion, and ask for Your continued blessings and guidance as I rest tonight. May Your name be a shield that wards off evil, may Your blessings be a beacon that guides me home, may Your wisdom and power be with me as I sleep, and may Your light shine upon me through all of life’s journeys.“
